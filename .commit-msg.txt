Code changes (st_coords.hpp):
  1. Boundary semantics documented in header comment:
     - s=0 → {0, 0.0}, s=totalLength → {lastSeg, lastSeg.length}
     - s at boundary → {nextSeg, 0.0} (upper_bound convention)
     - s<0 → clamped to {0, 0.0}, s>totalLength → clamped to {lastSeg, last}
  2. Thread safety documented: read-only after construction, no lazy init,
     no mutable caches. Naturally usable from multi-threaded mesh jobs.
  3. Separation of concerns documented: SegmentSequence does NOT provide
     sampleAdaptive(). Sampling owned by GeometrySegment, composition by
     Sequence, ownership+metadata by RoadV2.

     expectedHeading, actualHeading — editor can highlight problems
     without recomputing.
