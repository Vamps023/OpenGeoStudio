Add PBR lighting and sky gradient to Road Studio 3D viewport

Replaces the flat blue 3D viewport with Unreal/Blender-style PBR
lighting, a sky gradient background, and a ground reference grid.

Lighting:
- Per-vertex normal computation for all road meshes (AddQuads,
  AddPoly, AddColumn)
- Vertex struct extended with nx, ny, nz normal fields
- Directional sun light (warm 1.0, 0.95, 0.85) with diffuse shading
- Blinn-Phong specular highlights (specPower=32)
- Hemispheric sky ambient (sky from top, ground bounce from bottom)
- Fresnel-like rim lighting for edge definition
- Reinhard tone mapping + gamma correction
- 2D top-down mode uses flat colors (no lighting)

Background:
- Sky gradient shader: zenith blue (0.25, 0.45, 0.75) to warm
  horizon (0.65, 0.70, 0.78) to earth ground (0.35, 0.32, 0.28)
- Replaces flat blue glClearColor(0.1, 0.15, 0.3)

Ground grid:
- 10m grid cells extending ±200m around camera
- Semi-transparent (alpha=0.3) for subtle spatial reference
- Follows camera position dynamically

Shader changes:
- simple.vert: PBR lighting in vertex shader, passes normals
- simple.frag: unchanged (receives interpolated lit color)
- New sky gradient shader (inline in map_view_gl.cpp)
- New ground grid shader (inline in map_view_gl.cpp)

Build: SUCCESS (main app)
