Fix terrain tile selection to match Electron version

Three bugs fixed in TerrainStore::computeTileGrid():

1. Tile row direction was inverted
   Qt: row 0 at north (growing north-to-south)
   Electron: row 0 at south (growing south-to-north)
   Fix: Changed to south-to-north growth matching Electron

2. makeSquare() distorted geographic bounds
   Qt called makeSquare() which forces bounds to be square in
   geographic DEGREES. At high latitudes, longitude degrees
   represent less distance than latitude degrees, so this
   significantly expanded the selected area.
   Electron does NOT modify bounds after drag selection.
   Fix: Removed makeSquare() call

3. Tile grid was not forced to be square
   Qt used actual computed rows/cols (could be 3x5 rectangular)
   Electron forces square grid: rows = cols = max(required)
   Fix: Force square grid with actualTileWidth/actualTileHeight
   dividing bounds evenly, with last row/col edge case handling
