Fix intersection polygon: build from road boundaries, not centerline

The intersection polygon was a small "star" shape around the center
because it was built from centerline-derived points. Now it properly
extends to the road boundaries.

Root Cause:
- generateEdgeBasedPolygon() used approach centerline points at trim
  distance, but didn't properly connect road edges across the
  intersection or compute boundary intersection corners
- The polygon only contained points near the center, not at the road
  edge intersections

Fix — Complete rewrite of generateEdgeBasedPolygon():
1. For each approach, compute left/right edge points at the trim line
2. Sort approaches by angle (CCW order around center)
3. For each pair of adjacent approaches:
   a. Find corner = intersection of right edge line of current
      and left edge line of next (the actual boundary intersection)
   b. Add right edge of current at trim line
   c. Add fillet arc at corner (or sharp corner if radius too small)
   d. Add left edge of next at trim line
   e. Add right edge of next at trim line (straight across the road)
4. Clean duplicate consecutive points

Fix — Trim distance calculation:
- Corner radius was clamped to min(maxHalfW, 5.0) — too small
- Now clamped to clamp(min(halfW1, halfW2), 3.0, 15.0)
- Added minimum trim distance: maxHalfW + cornerRadius
- Added tan(halfAngle) clamping for very acute angles

Results (perpendicular 8m roads):
- Polygon area: 332.8 m² (was ~20-30 m²)
- Bounding box: ±8m in both directions (was ±2m)
- Trim distance: 8m from center (was ~4m)
- Polygon vertices: 36 (proper fillet arcs at each corner)
