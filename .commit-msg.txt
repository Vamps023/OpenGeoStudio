Fix 3D Studio crash — OGRE-Next cameras don't attach to SceneNodes

OGRE-Next 4.0 changed the camera API. Cameras are no longer
MovableObjects that get attached to SceneNodes. Instead, you
set position/lookAt directly on the camera.

The crash was:
  OGRE EXCEPTION(2:InvalidParametersException):
  Object already attached to a SceneNode or a Bone
  in SceneNode::attachObject

Fix:
  - Removed m_cameraNode and attachObject(m_camera)
  - Use m_camera->setPosition() and m_camera->lookAt() directly
  - Updated resetCamera() to use camera directly
  - Removed m_cameraNode from header
