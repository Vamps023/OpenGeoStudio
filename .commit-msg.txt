Phase 4h: Debug layer rendering

Implemented 8 of 18 debug layers with actual rendering in the 2D viewport:

1. Centerline (CL) — green thick line from engine centerline samples
2. LeftEdge (LE) — red line from computed left edge
3. RightEdge (RE) — blue line from computed right edge
4. LaneBoundaries (LB) — white dashed lane boundary lines
5. LaneBoundaryLines (LBL) — same as LB (alias)
6. SamplePoints (SP) — yellow dots at each centerline sample
7. LaneCenters (LC) — cyan lines at lane midpoints
8. RoadPolygon (RP) — magenta outline of the road polygon
9. MeshWireframe (MWF) — orange triangle wireframe from engine mesh

Remaining 9 layers (OffsetCurves, FilletArcs, TangentPoints, TrimPoints,
IntersectionPolygon, Triangulation, VertexNormals, UVGrid, LaneIds) are
defined but not yet rendered — they require deeper engine internals
that are not exposed through the public API.

Updated toolbar with 18 debug layer toggle buttons that appear when
debug mode is enabled (Ctrl+Shift+G). Each button is checkable and
toggles the corresponding layer.
