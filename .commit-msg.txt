Fix map tiles pruned immediately at low zoom levels

Root cause: pruneInvisibleTiles used a fixed maxDist of
  camZ * 2.0 + 2000 (e.g. 4000m at camZ=1000).

At low zoom levels (e.g. zoom 9), each tile covers ~78km.
The tile center is at ~39000m from the map center, which is
way beyond maxDist=4000. So every tile was pruned immediately
after being requested, causing an infinite loop of:
  request → reply → prune → request → ...

Fix: Changed prune logic to account for tile size.
  A tile is kept if: distance < viewRadius + tileHalfSize
  where viewRadius = camZ * 0.6 + 1000 (the visible area)
  and tileHalfSize = worldSize / 2

This ensures tiles that overlap the visible area are kept,
regardless of how large they are at low zoom levels.

Also removed all debug logging added during diagnosis.
