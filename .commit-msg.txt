Phase 1.8.4: Golden parity validation + formatVersion auto-dispatch

Proves that roadToV2() and roadToV2Legacy() produce mathematically
correct geometry. The conversion from legacy Road to RoadV2 is now
validated against captured golden fixture data.

Changes:
- roadToV2Auto(): format-version-aware auto-dispatch
  formatVersion >= 2 → roadToV2() (exact path)
  formatVersion < 2  → roadToV2Legacy() (legacy compatibility)
  Callers no longer need to decide which adapter to invoke.
- Documented formatVersion compatibility table in road_adapter.hpp
