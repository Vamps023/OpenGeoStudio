Implement SCANeR-style road creation tools in C++ engine

Add 6 road creation tools matching SCANeR Studio behavior:

1. Segment — straight line with tangent continuity
2. Circle Arc — constant-radius arc (G1 continuous)
3. Clothoid Arc — Euler spiral transition (G2 continuous)
4. Polyline — multi-point with optional fillet corner smoothing
5. Bezier — cubic Bezier with handle constraints
6. Clothoid Spline — G2-continuous multi-segment clothoid
