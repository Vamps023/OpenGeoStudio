Phase 1.8.5: roadFromV2() inverse adapter + round-trip verification

Completes the bidirectional conversion between legacy Road and RoadV2.
The full round-trip is now validated:

  Road → roadToV2() → RoadV2 → roadFromV2() → Road'

Key invariant:
  roadFromV2(roadToV2(Road))  is always LOSSLESS
  roadToV2(roadFromV2(RoadV2)) MAY be lossy once Phase 2 adds
  features that legacy Road cannot express (LaneSection, etc.)

Changes:
- ReverseAdapterReport struct: tracks lossless flag, warnings,
  segment counts (line/bezier/arc/spiral), approximated/unsupported
  segments
- roadFromV2() function: converts RoadV2 → Road
  - LineSegment → 2 corner ControlPoints
  - BezierSegment → 2 smooth ControlPoints with handles
  - ArcSegment → ControlPoint with SegmentMetadata(kind=Arc)
  - SpiralSegment → ControlPoint with SegmentMetadata(kind=Spiral)
  - Adjacent segments share boundary ControlPoints
  - All metadata preserved (id, name, color, profile, width, laneCount)
  - formatVersion set to 2
  - LaneSection (Phase 2) → warning + lossless=false
