Fix scene not loading after OGRE initialization (pending scene mechanism)

CRITICAL FIX: Scene was never actually loaded after OGRE init

Root cause: Studio3DWidget::onProjectOpened() scheduled scene auto-load
via QTimer::singleShot(500ms), but OGRE didn't initialize until 3D Studio
was activated at 1000ms. The scene load fired before OGRE was ready,
loadScene() returned early, and m_sceneAutoLoaded=true prevented retries.

Fix: Added pending scene mechanism to OgreWidget
- loadScene() now stores the scene JSON in m_pendingScene when OGRE
  is not initialized, instead of silently discarding it
- exposeEvent() checks for pending scene after OGRE initialization
  and loads it automatically
- This ensures the scene is always loaded regardless of timing
