Fix roads and objects to follow terrain elevation

Previously roads used a fixed Y offset from XODR elevation (10m + 2m = 12m)
and objects used a fixed Y=30 from the scene JSON. Neither followed the
actual terrain elevation, causing roads to float/sink and objects to hover.

Fix: Added sampleTerrainHeight(x, z) function that samples the stored
heightmap QImage at world coordinates (x, z) and returns the elevation.

Roads: Each road vertex now samples terrain height at its (x, z) position
and adds a small 2m offset to sit above the terrain surface.

Objects: Each object now samples terrain height at its (posX, posZ)
position and places its center at terrainY + scaleY/2 so the bottom
of the object sits on the terrain surface.

The heightmap QImage is stored as m_heightmapImage after loadTerrain()
and cleared in clearTerrain().
